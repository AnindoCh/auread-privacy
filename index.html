Auread Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

App: Auread (Android package com.C14thdesignstudio.firstread)

Developer: 14th Design

Auread is a reading app. You add your own documents (EPUB, PDF, Word, OpenDocument, RTF, FB2, text, Markdown or web pages) and read them as books. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

  • Your books and reading data stay on your device. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics.
  • Auread shows no forced ads. The only ad is an optional video you can choose to watch to support us. When you do, Google AdMob serves it and may process some device data, as described below.
  • Reading reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Information stored on your device

The following is created and kept only in Auread's private storage on your phone:

  • Documents you import. A copy is made inside the app so it can be paginated and read. The originals are not changed. Images and covers found inside a document are stored with it.
  • Reading data: your place in each book, bookmarks, reading time per day (for your streak and calendar), books finished and badges.
  • Preferences: the name you choose to be called, theme, room, text size, sound and reminder settings.

We cannot see any of this. It is not uploaded, synced or shared. Uninstalling Auread, or deleting a book in the app, removes it from your device.

Optional support videos (Google AdMob)

Auread never shows ads on its own. If you tap "Watch an ad to support us", the app starts Google's Mobile Ads SDK and shows one rewarded video. In order to deliver, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language),
  • ad interactions (for example, whether the video was shown or completed) and diagnostic data.

This processing is done by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form (UMP) asks for your choices before ads are shown; you can change them later in Settings → Privacy & ad consent. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

If you never tap the support button, the ads SDK is not started.

Notifications

If you turn on reading reminders, Auread asks Android for permission to post notifications and schedules them on your device. The messages may include the name you chose and your streak. No notification data leaves your phone. You can turn reminders off in Settings → Reading reminder or in Android's notification settings.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load the optional support video and to open links you tap (such as free books on Project Gutenberg).
  • Notifications: for the optional daily reading reminder.
  • Advertising ID: used only by the ads SDK for the optional support video.

Auread does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or your phone's other files. Documents are only read when you pick them or share them to Auread.

Children

Auread is a general-audience reading app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. Apart from the optional AdMob video described above, Auread does not send data to any third party.

Security

Your data stays in Auread's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Communication with Google's ad services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above.
